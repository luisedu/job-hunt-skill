# Interviews

- **AI-scored or async interviews** (audio bots, one-way video): follow the workspace's `interview/ai-interviews.md`. Offer a mock first: ask the likely questions one at a time and score each answer against its rules.
- **Numbers bank** in `interview/stories.md`: confirmed baseline → result figures. Interviewers, and especially bots, ask for comparisons. Every number comes from the user; record what they confirm in `profile.md` → "Confirmed in conversation".
- **Company prep:** create `interview/<company>.md` from `templates/interview/_company-template.md`: company facts with sources, role → evidence map, gaps and honest answers, questions to ask, pay plan.
- **Stories:** build reusable stories in `interview/stories.md` from confirmed facts.
- Before an interview, `insights.py companies --company X` shows the company's track record and known interview formats.
